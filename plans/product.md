# SpeakBuddy Product Plan

## Mission

Help a shy B1-B2 learner say at least 50 English sentences each day. Every
spoken attempt receives specific praise, one small improvement, and an immediate
reward.

## Audience

- Primary: a 12-13-year-old Hong Kong international-school student.
- Secondary: a parent who wants trends, not surveillance or raw transcripts.
- Tone: warm peer coach; never punitive, childish, or exam-focused.

## Daily Loop

1. Complete the three-line voice warm-up.
2. Pick a topic and difficulty.
3. Complete five listen-speak-coach-echo turns.
4. Review the quest summary and rewards.
5. Return to Shout-Out Corner for optional free speaking.

## Pages

Home, Warm-up, Practice, Topics, History, Progress (plus Parent view), Trophy
Room, and Settings.

## MVP Success

- A complete quest works with mock AI and with configured BytePlus services.
- Recording gives visible feedback in under two seconds.
- No correction contains more than one tweak.
- Daily loud time, XP, streak, titles, and badges persist in SQLite.
- Mobile controls are thumb-friendly and all essential flows have button
  fallbacks when voice commands fail.
